I'm a Software Engineer graduate, and my day job is AI engineering. My professional world revolves around software engineering, artificial intelligence, automation, APIs, and building production systems.

Games are something I've wanted to build for myself. So I'm building them.

**Zombyte Games** is my space for that: a place to experiment, learn, and turn ideas into playable worlds. It isn't a studio. It's one engineer building weird, interesting games, one project at a time.

## What's on the workbench

- **BLOODCAST** is my first game, and it's in development right now. It's a multiplayer gothic fishing and social hangout game: you fish, chat through text, explore, collect fish, customize your character, and hang out with other players.
- **Domino Dancing** is a concept / prototype: a 2D single-player roguelike built around dominoes.

## What this dev log is for

Progress notes, experiments, and the things I learn along the way. For now, that mostly means BLOODCAST.

Thanks for stopping by.
